How to play

  • Swap: exchange two rows.
  • Scale: multiply one row by a nonzero number.
  • Add/subtract: add a multiple of another row to the target row.
  • You get two undo tokens per level. Undo does not erase attempts.
  • Complete RREF to win. Stars depend on moves compared with the target.
